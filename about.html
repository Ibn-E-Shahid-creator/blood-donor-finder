About LifeLine

Why we built this and who can donate.

Our mission

In an emergency, families often spend hours calling relatives and posting on social media to find a donor. LifeLine puts willing donors and patients in one searchable place.

We do not sell blood or take payment. Donors give voluntarily.

Who can donate?

Age 18 to 60, weight above 50 kg, and in good health on the day of donation.

Men can donate every 3 months. Women can donate every 4 months.

If you have a fever, infection, recent surgery, or are taking certain medicines. Ask your doctor if unsure.
